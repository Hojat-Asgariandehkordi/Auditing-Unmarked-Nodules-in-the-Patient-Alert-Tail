\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Reference standards for lung nodule detection.}
LIDC collected annotations in two phases: each of four readers first marked a scan independently and then reviewed the anonymized marks of the others, without a forced consensus~\cite{armato2007variability,armato2011}. Agreement nevertheless remained incomplete, and reader sensitivity varies widely with the panel that defines truth~\cite{armato2009truth}. LUNA16 retained lesions accepted by at least three of the four readers as targets~\cite{setio2017}, and a subsequent reading of detector outputs identified further nodules accepted by all four new readers~\cite{jacobs2016cad}. We exploit this disagreement directly, using one reader's marks to define omissions and alternative reference rules to define alternative negative pools.

\paragraph{Sparse labels and missed lesions.}
DeepLesion is mined from clinical bookmarks~\cite{yan2018}, while Lesion-Harvester and EnrichedDL complete missing annotations from additional labels or model outputs~\cite{cai2021,ma2024}. Multi-reader methods model reader-specific labels or confusion matrices~\cite{tanno2019,wu2024}, and positive--unlabeled learning treats unmarked examples as a mixture of both classes~\cite{kiryo2017,bekker2018}; see~\cite{karimi2020noisy} for a survey of label noise in medical imaging. In contrast, we define omissions against a separate spatial reference and count the distinct lesions reached by an audit, including lesions that no high-scoring proposal covers.

\paragraph{Score-guided acquisition and corrected training.}
Multi-view and volumetric scores are standard for false-positive reduction in CT~\cite{setio2016views,setio2017}, and deep active learning has selected CT examples by uncertainty~\cite{liu2020active}. Two-phase sampling and active statistical inference use model predictions to direct labelling under a budget~\cite{chen2020sampling,zrnic2024active,li2025robust}. Known inclusion probabilities permit unbiased estimation~\cite{horvitz1952}, and augmented inverse-probability weighting with cross-fitting reduces its variance~\cite{robins1994,chernozhukov2018}; prediction-powered inference is closely related~\cite{angelopoulos2023ppi,ppssl2025}. Our design uses the within-scan rank and the low-alert threshold to decide where to sample, and measures what paired weak marks add at a fixed budget.

\paragraph{Calibration sets and evaluation labels.}
Conformal and order-statistic thresholds assume that calibration scores are exchangeable with those of the target population~\cite{angelopoulos2023conformal}, and recent work analyses the effect of noisy calibration labels~\cite{einbinder2024label,feldman2026corrupted}. Label errors in test sets can change which model ranks first~\cite{northcutt2021labelerrors}, and validation guidelines emphasize the quality of the reference and the choice of endpoint~\cite{maierhein2024metrics}. We quantify a related effect for patient alerts: which scans count as negative determines which maxima set the threshold, even when every candidate score is unchanged.
